# LANTERNBOUND — Hearts of the Hollow Dusk  (Story Bible)

Tone: warm, witty, bittersweet, hopeful. Think Octopath Traveler's character-first storytelling + a slow-burn romance
novel. Funny banter in the quiet moments, real stakes in the dark ones. NEVER grimdark. Love is the engine of the plot.

## Core theme
**Warmth is not a resource to be spent — it is something we make by sharing it.**
The villain believes love is fuel that burns people out. The heroes prove love *renews* when it is shared freely.

## The world: AUREN
Auren is lit by the **Hearthstar**, a lantern-sun built by the ancient Lampwrights and hung above the **Hearth Spire**.
Every person carries a **wick** — a tiny inner flame (you can see it as a faint glow in dim light, near the heart).
When a wick gutters from despair, grief or loneliness, the person turns **Hollow**: a quiet, empty shadow that drifts
toward the dark. The **Hollow Dusk** (a creeping gloom, purple-black cracks in the ground, hollow rifts) is spreading
because the Hearthstar is failing.

Secret: the ruling **Hearth Order** keeps the star alive by the **Tithe of Warmth** — lamps in every town quietly siphon
a little warmth from every person's wick to feed the Hearthstar. It is slowly burning the world's people out. The Order
tells everyone the lamps are a blessing ("Lanterns keep the Dark away").

Magic words: **Kindle** (to light/strengthen a wick), **Gutter** (to weaken), **Hollow**, **the Tithe**, **Lampwright**
(master craftsperson-mage of the old age), **Lamplighter** (a village lamp-tender), **wick**, **ember**.
Weather/time: the sun is a lantern, so the sky has a warm "lamplight dusk" feel even in daytime.

## Cast

### Rowan  (protagonist, player-named; default "Rowan") — "the Kindler"
19. Apprentice Lamplighter of Wickholm. Found as an infant on the village's doorstep. Rowan is **wickless**
("Cold") — villagers whisper it's an ill omen — yet Rowan's touch can **kindle other people's wicks**. Kind, stubborn,
curious, a little self-doubting, funny when nervous. NEVER use gendered pronouns for Rowan: use "you" (second person
dialogue) / "Rowan" / "they" only when unavoidable. Weapons: sword & staff. Element: light. Colors: warm gold + cream.
**TRUTH (revealed Ch5):** Rowan has no wick because Rowan carries the **Heart** — the Hearthstar's true core. Rowan's
mother, Lampwright **Elowen**, hid the Heart inside her infant child to stop the Order from using it, and gave up her own
wick to do it. That left her Hollow.

### Claret Vane  (romance option) — "the Tinker"   she/her
24. Captain-engineer of the steam-airship **Marigold**. Dark blue-black hair, aviator goggles on forehead, brown leather
jacket, blue top, brass everything. Sharp-tongued, practical, secretly very tender; shows love by fixing things.
Hates being helped, loves being understood. Father **Bram Vane**, shipwright, vanished into the Order's Cold Foundry.
Weapons: spear & axe (her "arc-lance" and a spanner-axe). Elements: lightning, fire. Likes: gears, honey, being listened to.
Catchphrases: "Don't touch that, it's load-bearing." / "I'm fine. The ship is not fine."
Arc: learns she doesn't have to carry everything alone; reunites with her father (who believes the Cold Engine will "end suffering").

### Caspian Thorne  (romance option) — "the Oathbreaker"   he/him
26. Former Knight-Lantern of the Hearth Order, now a gladiator in Gladius under a false name ("the Grey Lion"). Tall,
broad, silver-streaked dark hair, battered white-and-gold armor with the Order sigil scratched out. Courteous, honorable,
guilt-ridden: he left the Order after being ordered to put a village's lamps out "for the star" — leaving them Hollow.
Weapons: sword & spear. Elements: light. Role: protector — draws attacks, guards allies. Likes: old books, quiet, honest praise.
Arc: forgives himself; learns duty to people beats duty to institutions. Slow burn, formal, flustered by sincerity.

### Rook Calloway  (romance option) — "the Rogue"   he/him
27. Charming thief/gambler/con artist, flame-red hair, green coat, silver tongue, lucky coin. Always flirting, always
joking — it's armor: his whole village was drawn into the Hollow when he was a boy and he ran away instead of staying.
Terrified of being left (and of being the one who leaves). Weapons: dagger & bow. Elements: wind, dark.
Role: steals, evades, exploits weaknesses. Likes: lucky trinkets, ember wine, being chosen on purpose.
Arc: learns to stay.

### Seraphine "Sera" Whitlock  (romance option) — "the Cantor"   she/her
22. Cantor-priestess of the **Pale Choir** at Pale Abbey. Teal-green hair, white winged headdress, pale robes. Gentle,
earnest, funny in a dry deadpan way when comfortable. Her faith is cracking: she has begun to suspect the Order's hymns
and lamps hurt people. Weapons: staff. Elements: light; heals & buffs. Likes: lavender, sweets, honest doubts.
Arc: faith in people instead of institutions; finds her own voice (literally — she sings the Hollow back).

### Wren Alder  (romance option) — "the Hedge-Witch"   they/them
20. Whisperwood hedge-witch, nonbinary (they/them). Messy blue-grey hair, wide-brimmed blue witch hat with a feather,
patchwork cloak, speaks to plants/foxes, blunt and deadpan, wonderfully odd, secretly lonely. Stays in the forest because
people leave. Weapons: staff & bow. Elements: fire, ice, lightning (full caster). Likes: moonpetals, tea, being asked
questions, not being rushed. Arc: lets people into the garden.

### Allies / NPCs
- **Maren** — Wickholm's old Lampkeeper, Rowan's foster-parent. Gruff, loving, knits. Gives Rowan the **Ember Compass**.
- **Bram Vane** — Claret's father. Brilliant, broken, in the Cold Foundry.
- **Torvald** — Gladius Arena Master, loud, honest, sells tickets to everything.
- **Abbess Orla** — head of the Pale Choir, kind but compliant with the Order.
- **Inquisitor Hale** — Order enforcer, polite, relentless, hunts Rowan (mid-boss, Ch4, defeated, later redeemed in a line).
- **Pip** — a cheeky Lamplighter child in Wickholm and later Lumen Vale; comic relief and heart.

### Villains
- **Warden Ignatius Vael** — Archlampwright & head of the Hearth Order. Elegant, soft-spoken, silver coat with a lantern
  brooch. Lost his wife **Elowen** to the Hollow (he does not know *why*). Believes love is fuel that burns people out
  and grief is the real Hollow. Plan: seize the Heart (Rowan) to power the **Cold Engine**, which will *extinguish every
  wick* so no one can ever grieve again — "the Quiet". He sincerely believes this is mercy. Final boss (2 phases).
- **Hollow Elowen** — Rowan's mother, Vael's wife, a Hollow queen guarding the Spire's upper hall. Not evil; mourning
  shadow. Defeated by *kindling* her, not killing her. She briefly returns to herself: her one scene with Rowan is the emotional peak.

## Romance system (a MAIN pillar — treat it as such)
- Five candidates: **claret, caspian, rook, seraphine, wren**. Any can be romanced by any player (no gender lock).
- **Affection points** per candidate, 0–100. Tiers: 0 *Companion* (<15), 1 *Friend* (15), 2 *Close* (40), 3 *Devoted* (65), 4 *Beloved* (only after
  the confession; `lover` set). You can grow close to several, but there is only one Beloved.
- Earn points by: (a) **heart choices** in main-story chapters, (b) **Bond scenes** (heart-to-hearts on the Marigold deck; each
  candidate has 3 — `b1` Friend tier, `b2` Close tier, `b3` Devoted tier, each requires a minimum chapter), (c) **gifts**
  (each candidate loves/likes/dislikes certain items), (d) fighting together (Bond Skills, tiny trickle), (e) inn/campfire chats.
- **Bond Skills (battle):** at tier ≥2 Rowan can perform a duo skill with that partner ("Kindled Link: Claret"), stronger at higher tiers.
- **Festival of Lanterns (Ch4):** every candidate with ≥65 pts (tier 3) may ask Rowan to share a lantern. The player picks one (or "all of my
  friends"). The chosen one gets a **confession scene** (`<id>_confession`) → becomes the **Beloved** (`G.romance.setLover(id)`).
- **The Eve (Ch5):** each companion gets a short, intimate "night before the Spire" scene (`<id>_eve`) whose tone depends on whether they are the Beloved.
- **Endings (Ch6):** the Heart choice. Rowan's wick is the Hearthstar's Heart.
  1. **Twin Flame** (Beloved set, tier 4): the Beloved's wick is entwined with Rowan's; both survive; the star is relit by TWO hearts and the Tithe ends. One epilogue per candidate (`<id>_epilogue`).
  2. **Common Flame** (no Beloved, but ≥3 candidates at tier ≥1): every companion lends their flame; the star becomes a shared hearth. Warm friendship ending.
  3. **Lone Flame** (otherwise): Rowan becomes the new star alone — bittersweet, quiet, with a hint companions keep a lantern lit for them.
  The ending is chosen automatically from state at the final choice, but the player must *make the choice* (kindle / refuse the Quiet) in a scripted decision.

## Game feel notes
- The Marigold deck is the **home hub** (a small map): companions hang around; talk to them for bond scenes/gifts. A helm lets you pick destinations (Sky Chart).
- Lantern Waypoints (`obj_lamp`) = save + heal points; they appear in every dungeon & town.
- Each town has: an inn (romance campfire chat chance), a shop, 3–6 chatty NPCs with distinct voices, 2–4 chests, one secret.
- Humor beats in every chapter. Companions bicker lovingly. Rowan gets to choose tones ("Earnest", "Joking", "Flustered").
- Do not assume any specific companion is present in Ch2/Ch3 beyond Rowan and Claret (the player picks the order).
- Voice acting: NOT in this build (hooks only: `G.audio.voice(actorId, lineKey)` is a no-op stub).
